Step-by-Step Instructions: 1. Check the scene to ensure it is safe to perform CPR; you do not want to put your own life at risk. 2. If a person looks to be unresponsive, check to see if they are still breathing. Check responsiveness explicitly by tapping their shoulders and shouting, then checking for any response. 3. If the person does not appear to be able to respond, call 9-1-1 immediately and prepare actions for CPR. 4. Call someone directly to get an AED and first aid kit if available. 5. Kneel beside the victim and place them on a firm, flat surface to ensure maximum impact. Soft surfaces, like a bed or sofa, can reduce the effectiveness of CPR. 6. The following guidelines are from the American Red Cross: a. Rate: 100 to 120 chest compressions per minute, 30 at a time. b. Hands: two hands on the chest. c. Body: lock your elbows and put your shoulder directly over your hands. d. Compression: You want to go at least 2 inches deep into their chest. e. Allow the chest to recover to its normal position after every compression. 7. Prepare to give 2 breaths: a. Tilt the person’s head back until their head is slightly beyond its normal position to open their airway. b. Pinch their nose shut, inhale, and exhale while completely sealing your mouth over theirs c. Give each breath over 1 second and check to see if it makes the person’s chest rise. Give time for the air to exit before you repeat. d. *If the 1st breath doesn’t cause the chest to rise, readjust their head position and ensure you are sealing your mouth over them properly. If the 2nd breath doesn’t make the chest rise, something might be blocking their airway 8. Continue to give 30 chest compressions and 2 breaths. Use an AED as soon as you get one.
