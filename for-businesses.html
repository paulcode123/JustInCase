For businesses

Hands-on emergency preparedness training and free workshops for your team, workplace, or community organization.

Workplace & community

Emergency preparedness for your organization

Along with teaching students, Just In Case of Emergency helps businesses, community centers, and organizations feel more prepared when real emergencies happen. Choose the path that fits your needs below.

Our trainings are led by experienced paramedics, firefighters, law enforcement professionals, and military veterans — people who understand what it means to respond when every second matters.

Whether it’s CPR, choking, bleeding control, overdose response, or knowing what to do before emergency personnel arrive, our goal is simple: make life-saving skills clear, practical, and less intimidating for everyday people.

To request any of the services below, please fill out our Google Form, and someone from our team will reach out to you shortly.

CPR Certification

Need CPR certification for work, school, or just want to be prepared? JICE paramedics can come to your location and provide official American Heart Association (AHA) CPR certification. This hands-on class covers CPR, choking recognition and response, and basic emergency steps in a simple, easy-to-follow way.

Get an AED

Not sure what an AED is or how it works? You’re not alone. An AED is a life-saving device used during sudden cardiac emergencies. We can help your organization get one at a subsidized price and teach your team when and how to use it.

Risk Assessment Training

Have you ever called 911 and wondered what they’ll ask, what they’ll need, or what you should do while waiting for help? Our team can walk through your space, identify safety concerns, and help you build an emergency action plan so your staff feels more confident and prepared.

Posters & Manuals

We offer printed posters and manuals with simple, easy-to-read instructions for common emergencies, including adult, child, and infant choking, bleeding control, and more.

Additional Training

We also offer Stop the Bleed classes, accidental overdose and Narcan training, and other Emergency First Aid certifications.

Please feel free to contact us with any questions.

Request training

JICE hopes to educate people of all ages and backgrounds on emergency preparedness through our workshops. We work with schools, community groups, centers, and more to best provide our education.

Whether your audience is students, parents, seniors, volunteers, or community members, we work with you for free to create a workshop that fits your space, goals, and audience.

Hosts can choose from existing workshop ideas or design a custom workshop with us. Topics may include emergency preparedness, CPR awareness, basic first aid, bleeding control, choking response, family safety planning, and community safety.

If you are interested in hosting a JICE workshop, fill out our Google Form, and we will contact you shortly.
