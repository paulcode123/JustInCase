For businesses

Workplace readiness guidance and sponsorship options that connect your organization to community preparedness—at no cost for resource sign-up.

Partnerships

Resources and sponsorship

Each month we contact hundreds of businesses to offer free emergency readiness support and optional sponsorship. Partners receive paramedic-informed guidance, practical checklists and supply recommendations, and—when applicable—visibility on materials that reach workplaces and the public.

Emergency resources

Request assessments, recommended supply lists, manuals, and workplace-focused readiness steps aligned with how your team actually operates.

Sponsorship

Support our programs in exchange for recognition on printed resources, workshops, and community outreach—reaching businesses and individuals well beyond a single location.

Ask about free resources or sponsorship. We aim to reply within a few business days.
