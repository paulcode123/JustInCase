State chapters

Local chapters extend our workshops and resources so preparedness reaches every community—built around state-specific risks and partners on the ground.

Expansion

Local leadership, nationwide reach

Just In Case of Emergency is growing state chapters across the U.S. Each chapter adapts programming to regional hazards while staying aligned with our mission and materials.

Why chapters matter

Hazards and institutions vary by state. Chapters let us tailor outreach while deepening relationships close to home.

  • Align workshops with region-specific risks and seasonal preparedness priorities
  • Partner with schools, nonprofits, and agencies that serve local populations
  • Increase capacity in underserved areas through volunteer leadership
  • Represent JICE consistently while reflecting community context

If you care about preparedness and coalition-building in your state, chapter leadership may be a strong fit.
