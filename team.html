Our Executive Team

Just In Case of Emergency is run by youth leaders from different grades, schools, and backgrounds. However, we are all passionate about improving emergency preparedness in our distinct roles.

Serena Tsao

Co-Founder & Executive Director

Serena is a current junior at the Bronx High School of Science. She co-founded Just In Case of Emergency with professional paramedics to spread emergency preparedness to everyone. As the Executive Director, she oversees all teams and processes that keep JICE running.

Michelle Liu

HR Manager

Michelle is a junior at Berlin High School and is the HR Manager at Just In Case of Emergency. She oversees the personnel operations, bridging the gap between company leadership and employees to manage hiring, training, compensation, and workplace policies.

Sophie Jin

Content Director

Sophie is a sophomore at Bergen County Academies. As the Content Director, Sophie directs the article writing process and aims to educate individuals with the knowledge and confidence needed to act in emergency situations.

Aaron Shi

Fundraising Director

Aaron Shi is a sophomore at Manhasset Secondary School. As the Fundraising Director, he helps fund all of JICE's resources and mission by creating strategies to secure donations, grants, and events with the rest of the team.
