Who We Are

Prepared communities
are resilient communities.

Just In Case of Emergency is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to closing the gap in emergency education. From CPR and first aid to natural disaster response, we create accessible, engaging programs so everyone knows what to do when seconds count.

Our guides are clear, practical, and built for people of all ages — because emergencies don't discriminate, and neither does preparedness.

The People Behind It

Meet our team.

A passionate group of young leaders dedicated to making emergency preparedness accessible for every community.

in
Executive Director

Serena Tsao

Serena founded Just In Case with a simple belief: that knowing what to do in a crisis shouldn't be a privilege. She leads the organization's strategy, partnerships, and programs with a focus on equity-driven emergency education.

in
Programs Coordinator

Marcus Chen

Marcus designs and runs JICE's workshop curriculum, working with certified instructors to deliver first aid and disaster preparedness training that's engaging, practical, and age-appropriate for every audience.

in
Outreach Director

Aisha Patel

Aisha builds JICE's community partnerships and spearheads our outreach campaigns. She connects the organization with schools, local governments, and nonprofits to expand the reach of emergency preparedness education.

in
Volunteer Manager

Jordan Williams

Jordan recruits, trains, and coordinates JICE's growing volunteer network. Their work ensures every event runs smoothly and that volunteers feel empowered, valued, and equipped to make a real difference.
