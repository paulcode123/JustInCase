Who We Are

Prepared communities
are resilient communities.

Just In Case of Emergency is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded alongside real paramedics with one mission: making emergency preparedness accessible to everyone.

Emergencies don't discriminate. Neither does preparedness.
